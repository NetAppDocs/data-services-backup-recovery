=== Hyper-V workloads enhancement
In this release, NetApp Backup and Recovery introduces the following enhancement for Hyper-V workloads:

*NetApp MetroCluster support enhancements*: NetApp Backup and Recovery now enables you to protect Hyper-V VMs mirrored by NetApp MetroCluster and perform switchover and switchback operations.

For details about protecting Hyper-V workloads, refer to https://docs.netapp.com/us-en/data-services-backup-recovery/br-use-hyperv-protect-overview.html[Learn about protecting Hyper-V workloads with NetApp Backup and Recovery].
